Put Grid Finder on the home screen

iPhone (Safari or Chrome): tap Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app → Install.

Allow location when asked — it is needed only for “Where am I?”.

My places: connect once on each phone with your private setup link. On iPhone the home-screen icon keeps its own settings, so connect inside the icon.

Works without signal for X/Y; the map, postcode and address need internet. Addresses: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Accuracy: Ordnance Survey OSTN15 method, within about 0.2 m.
